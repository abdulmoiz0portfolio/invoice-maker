Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions About AutomatixInvoice

Direct, factual answers regarding pricing, templates, currencies, client storage, and PDF printing.

Is this invoice maker really free?

Yes. Generating, customizing, live mathematical calculation, client directory management, and exporting unlimited A4 PDF invoices is 100% free with no forced watermarks or signups required.

Can I add my own company logo?

Yes. Simply click 'Upload Logo' or 'Change Logo' in the left controls to add your PNG, JPEG, or SVG logo. Your logo is automatically saved with your drafts for future invoices.

Does it support multiple currencies and international taxes?

Yes. AutomatixInvoice supports over 50 global currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, AED, SAR, PKR, INR, etc.) and includes 1-click tax presets for US Sales Tax, UK VAT, EU VAT, and UAE TRN.

Can I save invoices for later and manage client contacts?

Yes. You can store your regular clients in the Saved Clients Directory and access all previous invoice drafts anytime via the Library Drawer stored securely in your browser.

How does the scannable payment QR code work?

Toggle the 'Scannable Payment QR' switch to embed a dynamic QR code linked to your PayPal, bank IBAN, UPI ID, or payment portal. Clients can scan it with their phone camera to remit funds instantly.

Can I duplicate invoices or set recurring intervals?

Yes. Use the 'Duplicate' button to clone an invoice with an auto-incremented invoice number (e.g. INV-1002), or enable 'Recurring' to advance billing dates on weekly or monthly schedules.

Freelancer & Agency Guide

Freelancer Invoicing Best Practices: How to Get Paid Faster

Sending professional invoices with clear itemized line items, explicit payment terms (such as Net 14 or Due Upon Receipt), tax registration numbers (EIN, VAT, NTN), and scannable payment QR codes reduces payment turnaround time by up to 40%.

📅 Explicit Due Dates

Always specify a concrete calendar due date rather than vague 'payment upon completion' terms.

🔢 Sequential Numbering

Maintain standardized chronological numbers (e.g. INV-1001, INV-1002) for friction-free tax audits and client accounting.

💳 Multiple Payment Routes

Provide bank wire details (IBAN/SWIFT) alongside instant payment QR codes for credit card or transfer apps.
